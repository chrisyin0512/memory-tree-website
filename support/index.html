How can we help?

For account help, timeline sharing, photo uploads, voice transcription, or App Store review questions, contact Memory Tree support.

  • Voice recording, transcription, and AI memory organization
  • Deleting memories or requesting account deletion

Account deletion

You can request account deletion by emailing support@memory-tree.app from the email address associated with your account. We may ask for confirmation before deleting account data.
